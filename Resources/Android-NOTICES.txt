macino Android support

MTP: libmtp 1.1.23 (LGPL 2.1 or later), libusb 1.0.30 (LGPL 2.1 or later).
These are unmodified upstream sources, compiled for arm64 macOS 14.0.
Shared libraries are loaded from the Android resource directory, not Homebrew.
Corresponding source archives are included in Android/Sources; build commands
and dependency hashes are in Scripts/build-android-tools.sh in the macino source.
License texts accompany this file. The libraries may be replaced or relinked.
https://github.com/libmtp/libmtp/tree/v1.1.23
https://github.com/libusb/libusb/tree/v1.0.30

Screen mirroring: scrcpy 5.0.1 official macOS arm64 distribution (Apache 2.0),
including its upstream adb executable and scrcpy-server. The archive's SHA-256
is pinned in the build script. Binary source and build instructions:
https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy/tree/v5.0.1
https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy/blob/v5.0.1/doc/build.md
https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy-deps
ADB is part of Android Open Source Project (Apache 2.0):
https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/modules/adb/
No device access or Android setting is automatically approved by macino.
