# macino 1.0.0

Your Mac’s everyday tools, a click away in the menu bar.

Check system stats, manage processes, open your favorite files, inspect network
activity, connect an Android phone or listen to a document offline. Clipboard
history is optional and opens with Control + V, or a shortcut you choose.

The Reader supports English and Persian. Its speed slider puts normal speed
right in the middle. The rest of the app and its documentation are in English.

## Install

Download **macino-1.0.0-arm64.dmg**, open it, then drag macino into Applications.
Open the installed app and look for its menu bar icon. Quit an older copy before
replacing it. Preferences are retained; clipboard history clears when you quit.

**Apple silicon · macOS 14+ · Locally signed, not notarized by Apple.**
Runtime compatibility has not been tested on every Mac and supported macOS version.
